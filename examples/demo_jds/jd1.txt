Junior Data Analyst
Example Retail — Melbourne

We are looking for a junior data analyst to join our reporting team. You will
clean and validate operational datasets, write SQL queries, automate recurring
reports with R or Python, document definitions, and explain findings to
non-technical stakeholders.

You have practical SQL skills, experience with either R or Python, careful
attention to data quality, and clear written communication. Graduate projects
and internships are welcome. Australian working rights are required; security
clearance is not required.

Salary: AUD 82,000–92,000 including super.
